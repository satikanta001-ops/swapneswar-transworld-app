🔁 Duplicate Challan Scanner

Finds challans with the same Challan No. + Truck + Date — usually caused by "Generate Challan" getting tapped twice before the first save landed. Keeps the oldest copy of each, removes the rest, and cleans up any duplicate auto-linked Cash Book entries those extra copies created.

👛 Cash Book Auto-Entry Cleanup

Checks every auto-linked ("🔗 Auto") Cash Book entry against the challan it's supposed to be tied to. Two things get flagged: orphaned entries, where the linked challan no longer exists (usually from deleting a challan before the "delete cleans up its Cash Book entry" fix existed) — these should be removed, since the challan they were charging is gone. And genuine duplicates — two auto entries pointing at the exact same challan.

Broken Verification Records

A verification batch is "broken" if the owner/bank record exists but its linked challans never got a real, usable payable amount — either never touched at all, or touched but left with an invalid amount (usually because destination weight was never entered before saving). These show as ₹0 batches in Payments even though the batch itself still shows a total. Deleting a broken record here removes that owner/bank entry AND resets any affected challan back to pending, so it shows up again in Challan Verification's search, ready to be verified properly this time.
